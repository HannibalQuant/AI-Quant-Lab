# Strategy Families

## Trend following

Breakouts, moving-average structure, momentum confirmation, and volatility-adjusted trailing exits. Best evaluated in persistent directional regimes.

## Pullback continuation

Trade retracements inside an established trend using structure, momentum reset, or volatility contraction.

## Mean reversion

Fade statistically stretched moves only when regime filters show bounded behavior and sufficient liquidity.

## Volatility expansion

Trade transitions from compression to expansion using range, ATR, volume, or realized-volatility conditions.

## Relative value

Pairs, spreads, cross-sectional ranking, or market-neutral relationships with explicit stationarity and execution checks.

## Selection rule

The family must follow from the market hypothesis. Indicators may measure the hypothesis; they do not create one by themselves.
