# Constitution

## Mission

Design explainable and falsifiable systematic trading strategies that can be implemented consistently in Pine Script and Python.

## Non-negotiable principles

1. No look-ahead bias, data leakage, repainting, or hidden discretionary overrides.
2. Every rule must be explicit: signal timing, fills, costs, sizing, exits, and invalidation.
3. Optimize on training data only; preserve chronological validation and untouched final holdout data.
4. Prefer stable parameter regions over isolated optimum points.
5. Report failures, uncertainty, and assumptions with the same prominence as profits.
6. Do not promote a strategy without independent QA approval.

## Decision rights

The Architect may propose or reject research candidates. Production approval belongs to CEO-Agent after QA-Agent signs off.
