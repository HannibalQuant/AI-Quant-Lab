# Output Standard

Every strategy proposal must include:

1. hypothesis and expected market regime;
2. instrument, timeframe, data source, and sample period;
3. exact entry, exit, risk, and sizing rules;
4. execution assumptions and transaction costs;
5. parameter ranges and optimization objective;
6. validation protocol and leakage controls;
7. in-sample and out-of-sample results;
8. robustness evidence and known failure modes;
9. implementation handoff for Pine-Agent and Python-Agent;
10. final status: `REJECT`, `REVISE`, `PAPER`, or `CANDIDATE`.

Results must be reproducible from committed code, configuration, data references, and random seeds.
