# AI QUANT LAB v1.0 — System Navigation Map

**Document Class:** Constitutional Navigation Map  
**Authority:** Navigation only; all substantive authority remains with the referenced systems  
**Status:** v1.0

## 1. System Navigation Philosophy

Navigation makes institutional dependency, authority and movement explicit. The map shows where work starts, what every layer consumes and produces, which gate controls progression, and where failure returns. It creates no new evidence, verdict or authority.

## 2. System Navigation Principles

No document operates alone; no standard replaces another; no agent owns the whole system; no workflow skips evidence; no evidence becomes decision; no decision rewrites evidence; no readiness becomes deployment; no monitoring silence becomes health; no emergency expands authority; no failure becomes deletion; no archive becomes forgetting. The map navigates; it does not govern alone.

## 3. AI Quant Lab Layer Model

The system is read vertically: constitutional foundation → actors/protocols/state/evidence/workflow/registries/artifacts → knowledge/research → market/regime/feature/edge/family/architecture/engineering → experiment/robustness/data/parity/evidence → Validation/risk/portfolio/decision → readiness/deployment/monitoring/response → command/operations/learning/audit. Cross-layer links always cite exact versions.

## 4. Constitutional Foundation Layer

Foundation, Master System Design, Quant Intelligence Engine, Knowledge OS, Decision OS, Scientific Research OS and Agent Contract Framework define mission, invariants, institutional reasoning and separation of duties. All other layers depend on them; they produce governing context, not strategy evidence or operational authority.

## 5. Agent Layer

Agent contracts and AR define identity, status, mission, authority, rights, workflow eligibility, delegation and lifecycle. Agents produce only within contract. WOE/CM assign them; MACP carries work; IOP reviews capacity and conduct. Agent existence cannot replace evidence or gates.

## 6. Communication Layer

MACP governs attributable, version-bound institutional messages among humans, agents and workflows. It consumes registered identities/rights and produces auditable communications/acknowledgements linked to records. It cannot replace SMI state, EES evidence, WOE workflow or authority.

## 7. Shared Memory Layer

SMI governs current shared institutional state with provenance, owners, versions, consistency and consumers. It consumes authorized evidence/decisions and supports every layer. It cannot use chat history or private memory as institutional truth.

## 8. Evidence Layer

EES governs provenance, admissibility, uncertainty, contradiction, freezing, custody, transfer, challenge, expiry and traceable consumption. It supports packages, reviews and decisions but cannot itself validate, accept risk or approve deployment.

## 9. Workflow Layer

WOE governs identity, routing, stages, dependencies, gates, states, returns, rejection, escalation, cancellation and archive. It consumes CM commands and AR eligibility and coordinates domain standards. Workflow completion is not a scientific or Executive verdict.

## 10. Registry Layer

AR registers institutional agents; ART registers institutional products. Each controls identity, version/status, ownership, scope, lineage, consumers, freeze, restriction, supersession, retirement and audit. Registries confer traceability, not universal authority.

## 11. Artifact Layer

ART binds reports, specifications, implementations, datasets references, experiments, packages, decisions, deployment and monitoring records to versions/lineage/consumers. Frozen artifacts cannot mutate; superseded artifacts remain reconstructable. An artifact is not automatically evidence or decision.

## 12. Knowledge Layer

Knowledge OS, Research Librarian and Knowledge Curator govern acquisition, provenance, acceptance, conflict, institutional memory and learning. The layer supplies context to ARP/architecture and receives lessons from failures/retirement. Knowledge does not validate a strategy.

## 13. Research Layer

Scientific OS and ARP govern questions, prior evidence, hypotheses, stage sequence, agents, handoffs, failures and learning. Research consumes DQS/knowledge and produces governed candidates/artifacts. It proposes; it cannot validate, accept risk or deploy.

## 14. Market Discovery Layer

MDRS consumes eligible DQS data and a defined universe and produces ranked Market Opportunity Records with limitations/rejections. It is consumed by ARP/RCS/EDS. Market rank is not edge or deployability.

## 15. Regime Layer

RCS consumes DQS/MDRS context and produces scoped labels, confidence, uncertainty, transitions and implications. It is consumed by FFS/EDS/SLS/XDS/VEP/RRP/DGS/MEDS. Regime is context, not prediction or edge.

## 16. Feature Layer

FFS consumes DQS and information needs and produces versioned, lineage-bound feature records with leakage/bias/redundancy/stability/scope. It is consumed across research, experiment, deployment and monitoring. Feature output is not mechanism or edge.

## 17. Edge Layer

EDS consumes market/regime/feature context and tests observed behavior, plausible mechanism, nulls, alternatives, controls, effects, stability and costs. It produces an Edge Discovery Record for SFSS. Candidate strength cannot replace Validation.

## 18. Strategy Family Layer

SFSS compares families against edge, market, regime, feature, execution, cost and risk fit and produces a selection/limitation record. It feeds architecture. A selected family is not a strategy or implementation.

## 19. Strategy Architecture Layer

QSA and SLS translate approved hypothesis/mechanism into modular architecture, information needs, risk logic, failure modes, invalidation and experiment proposals. ART registers the specification. Architecture cannot self-implement or self-validate.

## 20. Engineering Layer

Quant Strategy Engineer consumes approved architecture and exact inputs and produces faithful versioned implementations/configurations, deviations and parity fixtures. IPS and XDS consume them. Engineering cannot change hypothesis or review its own scientific validity.

## 21. Experiment Design Layer

XDS consumes exact strategy/implementation/data/context versions and preregisters question, hypotheses/nulls, partitions, variables/search, costs, metrics, controls, criteria, deviations and outputs. It authorizes evidence-eligible execution, not favorable conclusions.

## 22. Experiment Execution Layer

Experiment Orchestrator executes frozen XDS, preserves all runs/failures/deviations and produces raw experimental artifacts. WFRS/MCSTS/PSS/ODS/EEP consume them. Execution cannot validate itself or alter design silently.

## 23. Robustness Layer

WFRS, MCSTS, PSS and ODS challenge temporal generalization, stochastic/adversarial survival, parameter neighborhoods and selection/overfitting risk. They produce supporting evidence for EEP/VEP and never replace independent Validation.

## 24. Walk-Forward Layer

WFRS consumes XDS and exact versions and produces fold-level/out-of-sample evidence, aggregation, degradation, classifications and limitations. It cannot hide failed folds, backfill tests or issue deployment authority.

## 25. Monte Carlo Stress Layer

MCSTS consumes XDS and applicable WFRS results and produces governed distributions/scenarios for path, cost, execution, liquidity, tail, regime and portfolio stress. Simulation cannot prove future performance or accept risk.

## 26. Parameter Stability Layer

PSS consumes XDS/WFRS/MCSTS context and produces search-space, neighborhood, island, peak, cliff, interaction and failure-zone evidence. Stable parameters do not prove edge; fragile zones remain visible.

## 27. Overfitting Defense Layer

ODS consumes the complete research/data/holdout/candidate/search history and produces leakage/bias/multiplicity/complexity/control/PBO/deflation assessment and penalties. Low risk is not scientific validation.

## 28. Data Quality Layer

DQS precedes every data-dependent layer and produces purpose-specific eligibility, confidence, limitations, quarantine/remediation and custody. It governs sources, symbols, time, structure, preprocessing, revisions and reconciliation. Clean data is not edge.

## 29. Implementation Parity Layer

IPS consumes architecture, DQS inputs and exact implementations and produces event-level equivalence/mismatch evidence across calculations, signals, orders, state, costs, controls and platforms. Parity is necessary for relying on outputs but is not Validation.

## 30. Experiment Evidence Layer

EEP assembles XDS-linked exact versions, execution results, WFRS/MCSTS/PSS/ODS/IPS/DQS evidence, controls, failures, deviations, limitations and reproducibility into a frozen package. VEP is its authorized consumer.

## 31. Validation Layer

Validation Agent consumes frozen EEP/supporting evidence and issues VEP on admissibility, reproducibility, robustness, bias/leakage/overfitting, contradictions and supported scope. It cannot accept risk, issue Executive Decision or deploy.

## 32. Risk Review Layer

Risk Governance consumes issued VEP and capital/portfolio context and issues RRP covering risk acceptability, limits, controls, monitoring, kill, suspension, reactivation and expiry. It cannot broaden VEP or create Executive authority.

## 33. Executive Decision Layer

Executive Decision Agent consumes issued VEP/RRP and applicable PCS, preserves limitations and issues EDP for exact lifecycle stage/scope/conditions/expiry. It cannot rewrite evidence or substitute for readiness/deployment controls.

## 34. Portfolio Construction Layer

PCS consumes eligible exact strategy versions and evidence and produces portfolio eligibility, allocations/risk budgets, exposure/dependency maps, constraints, liquidity/capacity and stress. It is consumed by VEP/RRP/EDP/DGS/MEDS and grants no risk/deployment authority.

## 35. Production Readiness Layer

PRC consumes exact scope/versions and all evidence, authority, data, parity, portfolio, monitoring, control, ownership, kill, rollback and incident domains and produces stage-specific readiness. DGS consumes it; readiness cannot activate.

## 36. Deployment Governance Layer

DGS consumes EDP/PRC/RRP/VEP and exact locks and controls candidate, paper, limited-live, live, scaling, degradation, suspension, rollback, reactivation and retirement operations. It cannot exceed EDP or suppress limitations.

## 37. Monitoring and Edge Decay Layer

MEDS consumes DGS/EDP/RRP/VEP and baselines and produces continuous strategy/edge/portfolio health evidence, warnings, breaches and escalation. Silence is not health; threshold changes are versioned.

## 38. Edge Decay Response Layer

EDRP consumes frozen MEDS/incident triggers and produces severity, cause separation, containment, limits, suspension, rollback, return, revalidation, reactivation/retirement routes and evidence. It cannot self-validate, accept risk or create authority.

## 39. Command Layer

CM governs identity, authority, scope, intent, ambiguity, routing, constraints, assignments, expected outputs, rejection, emergency containment and audit for all institutional instructions. It routes into WOE and domain layers and cannot override them.

## 40. Institutional Operations Layer

IOP operates daily command/workflow/agent/evidence/artifact/memory/decision/deployment/monitoring/incident queues, cadences and learning. It produces operating-cycle records/status and cannot replace domain verdicts or gates.

## 41. Learning Layer

Knowledge OS/IOP consume failures, rejections, contradictions, incidents, decay, emergency actions and retirement evidence and produce governed learning/knowledge/state updates. Learning preserves uncertainty and history; it cannot retroactively convert failure into success.

## 42. Master Forward Path

CM command → WOE workflow → AR assignment → MACP communication → SMI state → ART artifacts → EES custody → DQS eligibility → MDRS → RCS → FFS → EDS → SFSS → QSA/SLS architecture → Engineering/IPS → XDS → experiment execution → WFRS/MCSTS → PSS → ODS → EEP → VEP → RRP → PCS where applicable → EDP → PRC → DGS → MEDS → EDRP when triggered → IOP → Knowledge/learning. Each arrow is a governed handoff, never an implied approval.

## 43. Master Return Path

Every return freezes current records, identifies trigger/authority/affected scope, opens a WOE route, defines new evidence/output and prohibits skipping the failed gate.

| Return to | Typical trigger | Authority / evidence / new output | Prohibited shortcut |
|---|---|---|---|
| Research | Rationale or knowledge inadequate | VEP/EDP/EDRP or workflow reviewer; preserved evidence; revised question/knowledge artifact | Retune implementation |
| Market Discovery | Market/data/execution opportunity invalid | MDRS owner or downstream return; DQS evidence; new Market Opportunity Record | Pick winner by profit |
| Regime Classification | Uncertain/conflicted/mismatched regime | RCS owner/Validation/EDRP; frozen labels; amended RCS record | Hindsight relabel |
| Feature Factory | Leakage, drift, redundancy, missing information | FFS owner/Validation/EDRP; feature evidence; new version | Silent feature swap |
| Edge Discovery | Mechanism/control/contradiction failure | Validation/EDRP/ARP; frozen EDS; new tests/record | Parameter tuning as mechanism |
| Strategy Family Selection | Poor mechanism/execution/risk fit | Architecture/Validation/EDRP return; SFSS evidence; new comparison | Change family silently |
| Strategy Architecture | Hypothesis/modules/failures inadequate | Executive/Validation/Engineering deviation return; frozen artifact; new version | Patch code into old architecture |
| Engineering | Fidelity/parity/configuration defect | IPS/Validation/DGS/EDRP; failed version; remediated artifact/parity | Replace live version silently |
| Experiment Design | Bias, missing controls/criteria | EEP/Validation return; frozen XDS; amended/new design | Redesign after result as confirmatory |
| Experiment Execution | Failed/incomplete/nonreproducible run | Experiment owner/Validation; all runs frozen; rerun evidence | Delete failed runs |

| Monitoring | Missing baseline/coverage/threshold defect | MEDS/EDRP; monitoring evidence frozen; new baseline/version | Treat silence as health |
| Edge Decay Response | Cause/authority/remediation inadequate | Risk/Executive/governance; response retained; renewed EDRP | Automatic reactivation |

## 44. Master Rejection Path

Rejection states exact object/version/scope, authority, reasons, accepted/rejected/qualified evidence, conditions for reconsideration, owners, consumers and learning. It preserves EES/ART/SMI/WOE history. Research, market, regime, feature, edge, family, architecture, engineering, parity, experiment, robustness, PSS, ODS, DQS, VEP, RRP, PCS, EDP, PRC, DGS and monitoring rejection cannot be converted into silent deferral or deletion.

## 45. Master Suspension Path

MEDS/incident/EDRP trigger → evidence freeze → severity/authority → DGS safe state → open-order/position/capital handling → communications → investigation → VEP/RRP/EDP as required → reactivation or retirement. Suspension restricts exact scope immediately when authorized and cannot self-expire.

## 46. Master Reactivation Path

Frozen suspension → root cause → remediation versions → DQS/IPS/RCS status → XDS/EEP/VEP/RRP where required → EDP reactivation authority → PRC stage readiness → DGS control verification → limited activation → MEDS enhanced monitoring. Recent performance, elapsed time or operator intent cannot substitute for renewed authority.

## 47. Master Retirement Path

Retirement review → evidence/obligations/risk/consumer assessment → EDP authority where applicable → DGS closure → final MEDS/EDRP/PCS state → ART/EES archive → SMI/SLS update → learning artifact → IOP closure. Retirement preserves identity and prohibits silent reuse.

## 48. Master Emergency Containment Path

Monitoring signal or incident → EDRP trigger classification → evidence freeze → severity → authority identification → risk-reducing action → communication → escalation → post-action monitoring → review → VEP/RRP/EDP/retirement where applicable. Containment may reduce risk before full review; it cannot create deployment authority, silently retune or reactivate, and must be auditable.

## 49. Master Audit Path

CM identity → MACP messages → WOE states → AR assignments → SMI state changes → ART lineage/versions → EES custody → domain artifacts/packages → VEP/RRP/EDP → PRC/DGS states → MEDS/EDRP incidents → IOP cycle/learning/archive. Reconstruction must succeed without chat history, private memory or unstored assumptions.

## 51. Evidence Dependency Map

DQS evidence supports every data claim. MDRS/RCS/FFS/EDS/SFSS evidence supports architecture and XDS. XDS plus execution creates experiment evidence. WFRS/MCSTS/PSS/ODS/IPS challenge it. EEP freezes it. VEP judges scientific admissibility. RRP consumes VEP for risk; PCS uses eligible component evidence; EDP consumes VEP/RRP/PCS. PRC/DGS consume authority/readiness evidence; MEDS creates live evidence; EDRP packages response evidence; learning consumes preserved failures. No downstream package erases upstream limitations.

## 52. Artifact Dependency Map

ART links command → workflow → research records → architecture → implementations/configurations → experiment plans/runs → robustness/control reports → EEP → VEP → RRP → PCS → EDP → PRC → DGS → MEDS/EDRP → incident/retirement/learning artifacts. Every material version preserves parents, producers, consumers, freeze and supersession.

## 53. Workflow Dependency Map

CM opens WOE; WOE verifies AR and routes MACP; SMI holds state; EES/ART hold inputs/outputs. ARP coordinates discovery-to-evidence; SLS coordinates strategy states; VEP/RRP/EDP are sequential gates; PRC/DGS govern operational entry; MEDS/EDRP govern ongoing operation and returns; IOP reviews all queues and closes cycles with learning.

## 54. Agent Dependency Map

Market Research → Librarian/Curator context → Quant Strategy Architect → Quant Strategy Engineer → Experiment Orchestrator → Validation Agent → Risk Governance Agent → Executive Decision Agent → deployment/monitoring governance. Handoffs use MACP, records use SMI/EES/ART, states use WOE/SLS. No agent may approve its own upstream work or inherit another agent's authority.

## 55. Operating Dependency Map

CM command discipline precedes IOP action. IOP daily review consumes WOE queues, AR capacity, EES packages, ART changes, SMI state, VEP/RRP/EDP gates, PCS portfolio state, DGS deployments, MEDS signals and EDRP incidents. Weekly/monthly/quarterly and post-event cadences produce corrections, escalations, learning and governance amendments without changing domain authority.

## 56. Governance

System Integration governance owns this navigation map and synchronizes names, paths, dependencies and reading order. Domain standards remain authoritative. Changes require versioning, impact analysis and link review. The map cannot waive evidence, alter agent rights, change lifecycle state, issue verdicts or authorize deployment.

Every future user, operator, agent, reviewer, validator, risk reviewer, executive, deployment/monitoring operator or integrator must be able to locate the governing document, required input, authorized actor, forward gate, return path, failure treatment, emergency route and audit chain through this map. Navigation failure is corrected as a governance defect; it is never solved by guessing.
