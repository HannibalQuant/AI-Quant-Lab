# AI QUANT LAB — Overfitting Defense Standard v1.0

**Document Class:** Constitutional Standard  
**Authority:** AI Quant Lab Evidence and Validation Governance  
**Status:** v1.0  
**Applies To:** Every institutional edge, feature, parameter, experiment, robustness claim, validation candidate, risk review, deployment candidate, and monitoring baseline

## 1. Overfitting Defense Philosophy

Overfitting defense protects the institution from mistaking adaptation to observed research history for persistent market knowledge. It governs the entire discovery path, not merely the final model. Favorable performance has no standing when its search burden, information exposure, alternative candidates, failed trials, leakage risks, and uncertainty are hidden.

## 2. Overfitting Defense Principles

No strategy advances without overfitting review. No review exists without governed design, search history, feature and parameter history, data-access history, multiple-testing awareness, leakage review, holdout protection, and negative controls. Best backtests, best trials, and selected survivors cannot establish edge. Failed defense remains learnable; defense is neither Validation, risk approval, nor deployment authority.

## 3. Overfitting Risk Identity Model

Each review receives immutable Overfitting Risk ID and version linked to exact strategy, implementation, experiment, data, market, timeframe, regime, feature, edge, family, WFRS, MCSTS, and PSS versions. Identity survives return, rejection, challenge, supersession, and archive.

## 4. Overfitting Risk Object Model

The record shall define owner and responsible agent; all linked identities; scope and observation period; research, data-access, holdout-access, candidate, search, optimization, rejection, failure, selection, and metric histories; primary and secondary metrics; complexity sources and degrees of freedom; multiple-testing sources and correction; leakage, bias, control, performance-deflation, PBO, classification, confidence, maturity, limitations, contradictions, deviations, follow-up, MACP source, SMI/EES/artifact/workflow links, timestamps, and audit trail.

## 5. Overfitting Defense Lifecycle

**Proposed → Scope Received → Risk Sources Registered → Bias Review Started → Leakage Review Completed → Multiple Testing Review Completed → Control Tests Registered → Complexity Review Completed → Negative Controls Reviewed → Penalty Review Completed → Overfitting Classified → Evidence Packaged → Returned → Rejected → Challenged → Superseded → Archived**

Transitions require owner, authority, inputs, rationale, outputs, timestamp, and prior-state reference. Return and challenge preserve the prior version.

## 6. Overfitting Risk Scope Rules

Scope shall declare the claim, strategy and implementation versions, datasets, dates, markets, timeframes, regimes, features, parameters, metrics, candidate population, research stages, and intended reliance. Unrecorded expansion invalidates the classification for the added scope.

## 7. Data Mining Risk Rules

All searches through observations, labels, transformations, thresholds, markets, periods, or outcomes count as data mining exposure. The review records purpose, sequence, decision maker, retained and discarded outcomes, dependency, and cumulative false-discovery burden.

## 8. Multiple Testing Risk Rules

The effective candidate population includes formal trials and manual, exploratory, interrupted, renamed, rerun, or selectively reported variants. Correlated tests are not treated as independent, but correlation never permits their omission.

## 9. Leakage Risk Rules

Information crossing an authorized temporal, sample, label, preprocessing, feature, model-selection, validation, or operational boundary is leakage. Suspected leakage is a block until bounded; material leakage invalidates affected evidence.

## 10. Look-Ahead Bias Rules

Every input, transformation, label, timestamp, revision, fill assumption, and decision must have been available at the governed decision time. Future availability, revised data, centered transformations, or retrospective alignment must be isolated or rejected.

## 11. Survivorship Bias Rules

Universe construction shall preserve delisted, failed, inactive, unavailable, and changing constituents relevant to the period. Current survivors cannot represent a historical opportunity set without explicit qualification and downgrade.

## 12. Selection Bias Rules

Selection of samples, venues, assets, periods, regimes, trades, folds, seeds, scenarios, or reports must be preregistered or fully disclosed. Favorable selection after outcome observation is exploratory and cannot retain confirmatory status.

## 13. Hindsight Bias Rules

Hypotheses, mechanisms, criteria, exclusions, and expected failure behavior are compared with timestamped preregistration. Post-result explanations are labeled retrospective, versioned, and independently retested.

## 14. Narrative Fitting Rules

An attractive causal story cannot rehabilitate weak, unstable, contradicted, or mined evidence. Narrative changes following results are recorded as additional researcher degrees of freedom and require new falsifiable tests.

## 15. Feature Overfitting Rules

All proposed, transformed, combined, rejected, and retained feature versions remain visible. Feature selection must account for leakage, redundancy, regime dependence, temporal instability, ablation results, and selection multiplicity under FFS.

## 16. Parameter Overfitting Rules

Parameter histories include search spaces, changes, fixed and conditional values, rejected regions, manual choices, seeds, stopping, and selection rules. Isolated peaks, cliffs, unstable interactions, and post-result narrowing invoke PSS downgrade or rejection.

## 17. Regime Overfitting Rules

Regime taxonomy, thresholds, confidence, transition rules, and exclusions must predate evaluated outcomes. Relabeling regimes to preserve results is prohibited; uncertain, mixed, and failed regimes remain in evidence.

## 18. Market and Timeframe Overfitting Rules

Every considered market and timeframe belongs to the candidate population. Selection must preserve MDRS rationale, data and cost differences, rejected candidates, transfer limits, and whether the chosen scope was known before performance.

## 19. Optimization Overfitting Rules

Optimization intake requires full search space, objectives, constraints, pruning, seeds, trial count, stopping rules, all trials, failed trials, manual interventions, selection route, and holdout contacts. Optimizer rank is not evidence rank.

## 20. Walk-Forward Overfitting Rules

WFRS designs are reviewed for post-result windows, selected folds, repeated redesign, reoptimization leakage, favorable aggregation, and repeated access to tests. Walk-forward can itself be overfit; one chosen schedule cannot prove temporal robustness.

## 21. Monte Carlo and Stress Overfitting Rules

MCSTS review covers scenario selection, perturbation redesign, seed replacement, dependency destruction, favorable severity, excluded failures, and threshold tuning. Stress evidence must not be tailored until the tested strategy survives.

## 22. Parameter Stability Overfitting Rules

PSS evidence is challenged for cherry-picked islands, post-result neighborhood definitions, hidden boundaries, excluded fragile zones, selected metrics, and interaction omission. A smooth map can still be mined from many maps.

## 23. Robustness Claim Overfitting Rules

Robustness claims disclose every attempted robustness method, specification, dataset, fold, scenario, seed, threshold, and exclusion. Robustness among selected survivors or selected tests is not institutional robustness.

## 24. Negative Control Rules

Controls are registered before interpretation with identity, purpose, inputs, transformation, preserved and destroyed structure, randomization and seed policy, metrics, expected failure, thresholds, interpretation boundary, contradictions, blocking conditions, limitations, and reproducibility material.

## 25. Randomized Signal Control Rules

Signal timing or assignment is randomized while explicitly preserving declared marginal properties. Similar performance to the original weakens specificity and blocks progression until explained.

## 26. Shuffled Return Control Rules

Shuffling shall declare which temporal, regime, clustering, cross-sectional, and tail dependencies it destroys. Persistence of the claim under destroyed required structure contradicts its mechanism.

## 27. Time-Shifted Feature Control Rules

Features are shifted across defensible lags and leads to expose alignment errors, leakage, and spurious timing. Implausible future or unrelated shifts retaining performance trigger investigation or invalidation.

## 28. Destroyed Temporal Structure Control Rules

The control destroys ordering or dependence required by the proposed edge while preserving agreed distributions. Survival means the test may reward distributional artifacts rather than the claimed mechanism.

## 29. Placebo Regime Control Rules

Random, permuted, or irrelevant regime labels are compared with governed RCS labels. Similar or superior results weaken regime dependence and prohibit regime-specific claims without new evidence.

## 30. Unrelated Market and Timeframe Control Rules

Unrelated scopes test specificity and accidental universality. Transfer can be genuine only with a recorded mechanism; unexplained persistence or arbitrary winner selection increases mining risk.

## 31. Complexity Burden Rules

Review counts parameters, conditional logic, features, redundancy, branches, regimes, adaptive and hybrid components, thresholds, filters, optimization freedoms, manual decisions, repeated cycles, exclusions, undocumented tuning, interactions, and interpretability, validation, and monitoring burden. Greater complexity requires disproportionately stronger independent evidence.

## 32. Degrees of Freedom Rules

Degrees of freedom include machine-selected and human-selected choices. Hidden discretion, alternate preprocessing, flexible stopping, report selection, narrative changes, and choice among methods are counted even when absent from a formal parameter vector.

## 33. Multiple Comparison Adjustment Rules

Adjustment must match the effective number, dependence, sequencing, and adaptivity of comparisons. The method, assumptions, family of claims, residual false-discovery risk, and reasons for non-adjustment are explicit; absence of defensible correction forces limitation or downgrade.

## 34. Performance Deflation Rules

Headline performance is deflated for tested candidates, parameters, features, markets, timeframes, regimes, metrics, holdout reuse, selection path, trial dependence and correlation, metric volatility, tails, uncertainty, and false-discovery risk. Raw values remain visible but cannot replace deflated conclusions.

## 35. Probability of Backtest Overfitting Rules

PBO assessment shall identify candidate population, performance ordering, partition logic, rank degradation, assumptions, dependence, uncertainty, and limitations. PBO is one diagnostic, never a cleansing certificate or substitute for controls and true out-of-sample evidence.

## 36. Deflated Sharpe and Metric Correction Rules

Deflated Sharpe or analogous correction shall declare metric definition, non-normality and autocorrelation treatment, sample uncertainty, multiplicity estimate, benchmark distribution, and selection effects. Correction applies to the declared metric only and cannot legalize unrelated claims.

## 37. Out-of-Sample Integrity Rules

Out-of-sample evidence must be chronologically and decisionally untouched until all relevant choices are locked. Any direct or indirect use for tuning, selection, explanation, stopping, or redesign consumes independence and is recorded.

## 38. Holdout Protection Rules

Each holdout has identity, custodian, purpose, eligibility, access authority, access log, release condition, and exhaustion status. Results are disclosed once under the registered decision rule; repeated exposure requires new evidence or explicit downgrade.

## 39. Research Reuse and Repeated Access Rules

Institutional memory carries prior exposure forward across agents, renamed strategies, forks, features, markets, and experiments. Reuse never resets the research clock. Cumulative contact determines whether data remains exploratory, validation, or holdout material.

## 40. Overfitting Penalty and Downgrade Rules

Penalties may reduce evidence weight, confidence, maturity, admissible scope, or verdict and may require new data, frozen redesign, simpler architecture, independent replication, or rejection. Material leakage, concealed search, untraceable path, or surviving invalid controls mandates failure or invalidity, not a cosmetic caveat.

## 41. Overfitting Classification Model

Permitted classifications are **LOW OVERFITTING RISK**, **MODERATE OVERFITTING RISK**, **HIGH OVERFITTING RISK**, **INCONCLUSIVE**, **FAILED OVERFITTING DEFENSE**, and **INVALID**. Each states scope, rationale, controls, penalties, contradictions, and allowed reliance. Low risk never proves edge or future performance.

## 42. Overfitting Confidence Model

Confidence is **HIGH**, **MEDIUM**, **LOW**, **INSUFFICIENT**, or **CONFLICTED** and reflects completeness, custody, review independence, control quality, sample relevance, reproducibility, and contradiction—not strategy performance.

## 43. Overfitting Maturity Model

Maturity is **DESIGNED**, **EXECUTED**, **EVIDENCE_READY**, **VALIDATION_CANDIDATE**, **RISK_REVIEW_CANDIDATE**, **RETURNED**, **REJECTED**, or **SUPERSEDED**. Maturity describes process completion, not favorable evidence.

## 44. Deviation and Amendment Rules

Deviation records identify original rule, event, timing, knowledge available, owner, effect, exploratory downgrade, affected evidence, corrective action, and approval. No amendment is retroactive. Material amendments create a new version and ordinarily require fresh untouched evidence.

## 45. Overfitting Evidence Packaging

The frozen package includes identity/version; XDS and applicable WFRS, MCSTS, PSS; strategy and implementation versions; full contexts and data version; research/data/holdout histories; complete candidate population; failed trials and rejected features, parameters, markets, timeframes, regimes, and metrics; complexity and degrees of freedom; multiple-testing analysis and correction; controls and outcomes; performance deflation; PBO; leakage and bias reviews; classification, confidence, maturity, limitations, contradictions, prohibited interpretations, and follow-up.

## 46. Overfitting Audit and Reconstruction

An independent reviewer must reconstruct what was known, tried, accessed, selected, rejected, changed, and reported at every decision point without chat history or private memory. Audit preserves authorship, custody, hashes or governed identity references, versions, timestamps, transitions, access events, exceptions, and consumers.

## 47. Integration with MACP

MACP carries identity-bound intake, challenge, return, breach, classification, handoff, and revocation messages. Messages cannot replace registered records and must preserve exact versions, scope, authority, conditions, and evidence links.

## 48. Integration with SMI

SMI stores governed current state, exposure history, blockers, classifications, conditions, and references. Overfitting conclusions are never inferred from unstored agent memory; conflicts trigger WOE blocking and reconciliation.

## 49. Integration with EES

EES governs provenance, admissibility, uncertainty, contradiction, chain of custody, freezing, challenge, and consumer traceability. Control and adverse evidence travel with the claim and cannot be silently detached.

## 50. Integration with WOE

WOE enforces intake, independence, stage gates, owners, dependencies, returns, escalation, rejection, archive, and re-entry conditions. Failed controls, missing histories, leakage, or exhausted holdout block advancement.

## 51. Integration with Agent Registry

Only registered agents with explicit review, message, memory, evidence, and workflow rights may create, challenge, classify, or consume defense records. Capability does not grant authority; no agent may approve its own concealed research path.

## 52. Integration with Artifact Registry

ART registers designs, logs, histories, controls, results, corrections, verdicts, and packages with lineage, owner, consumer, version, freeze, supersession, retention, and reconstruction status. Silent artifact mutation invalidates reliance.

## 53. Integration with Strategy Lifecycle Standard

SLS advancement beyond experimentation requires a scope-matched defense record. Material strategy, feature, parameter, data, market, timeframe, regime, objective, or selection changes trigger review and may require renewed untouched evidence.

## 54. Integration with Autonomous Research Pipeline

ARP preserves the complete discovery population and return loops from market discovery through monitoring. Stage changes, substitutions, failures, and rejected branches remain visible so autonomy cannot erase researcher degrees of freedom.

## 55. Integration with Experiment Design Standard

XDS supplies preregistered hypotheses, nulls, scope, variables, data partitions, metrics, controls, criteria, costs, assumptions, deviations, and interpretation boundaries. Missing or retrospective design prevents confirmatory classification.

## 56. Integration with Walk-Forward and Robustness Standard

WFRS supplies all window plans, folds, reoptimization paths, exclusions, aggregations, failures, and redesigns. ODS evaluates selection across walk-forward specifications and preserves temporal exposure history.

## 57. Integration with Monte Carlo and Stress Testing Standard

MCSTS supplies perturbations, preserved and destroyed dependencies, seeds, simulations, scenarios, exclusions, thresholds, and failures. ODS challenges scenario mining, seed selection, and post-result severity changes.

## 58. Integration with Parameter Stability Standard

PSS supplies search space, neighborhoods, islands, peaks, cliffs, interactions, adverse regions, and selection history. ODS assesses whether stability itself was selected from a larger undisclosed search.

## 59. Integration with Experiment Evidence Package

EEP must include or reference the frozen ODS package, all adverse controls, penalties, and limitations. Incomplete histories, selective reporting, or unresolved blocks make evidence not Validation Ready.

## 60. Integration with Validation Evidence Package

VEP independently assesses ODS admissibility, reproducibility, bias, leakage, multiplicity, stability, contradictions, and limitations. Validation may narrow, return, require evidence, or reject; it cannot rewrite ODS evidence.

## 61. Integration with Risk Review Package

RRP receives classification, residual uncertainty, scope, complexity, unstable dependencies, exhausted holdouts, controls, and revalidation triggers. Risk cannot broaden ODS or Validation scope and cannot convert uncertainty into scientific validity.

## 62. Integration with Deployment Governance Standard

DGS verifies exact reviewed versions, authorized scope, conditions, controls, monitoring, and expiry. Version mismatch, unreviewed adaptation, or expired reliance blocks or suspends deployment.

## 63. Integration with Monitoring and Edge Decay Standard

MEDS monitors feature, parameter, regime, complexity, selection, and performance drift against reviewed assumptions. Live retuning, repeated threshold changes, monitoring-driven mining, or baseline replacement triggers new ODS review rather than automatic promotion.

## 64. Governance

The Validation governance authority owns this standard; independent Validation controls classification, while Experimentation supplies complete histories and cannot self-clear. Changes require impact analysis, versioning, approval, migration rules, and prospective effect. Exceptions are scoped, time-bounded, owned, auditable, and cannot waive lineage, leakage, holdout, control, multiplicity, or reconstruction duties.

Mandatory risk types include data mining, multiple testing, look-ahead, information/label/feature/target/preprocessing leakage, survivorship, selection, confirmation, hindsight and narrative bias, regime/market/timeframe/feature/parameter/metric selection, optimization/WFRS/MCSTS/PSS/robustness overfitting, holdout reuse, data snooping, complexity overfit, and degrees-of-freedom inflation.

Every future AI Quant Lab edge, feature, optimization, parameter, robustness, experiment, validation, risk, deployment, or monitoring claim must conform to ODS before institutional reliance. Cancellation, rejection, invalidation, supersession, or retirement never erases the research path.
